# Mayor's Surveyor

A free city planner for SimCity BuildIt that I made because I wanted to try out layouts
before spending hours rebuilding in the game. It runs in your browser, no install needed.

**Open it here:** https://2030mayor.github.io/Mayor-s-Surveyor/

## What it does

- All six regions, with their land, beaches, mountains and fixed roads/rails
  shaped like the game's.
- Place buildings from the full catalog, and rotate, copy, move or delete them.
- Draw roads and rails. Like in the game.
- See service coverage and population boosts on the map.
- The City Report shows population, services, capacity and what's not connected.
- Make your own custom buildings, or design your own region.
- Save as many plans as you like, and export/import them as files.
- 18 languages.

Your plans are saved in your own browser and never uploaded anywhere. If you clear your
browser data they're gone, so export the ones you care about.

## Honest notes

- **It's built and tested mostly on desktop (Chrome).**
- **Phones:** supposed to work, did not test it thoroughly.
- I'm one person doing this for fun, so expect the odd bug.
